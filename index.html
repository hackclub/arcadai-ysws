USING AI?

Why Are We Using AI?

Games can take weeks to build from scratch. And AI is a great tool to learn if used in the right way. So for ArcadeAi, we want to encourage participants to use AI to build the first version of their game, then improve it with their own ideas and creativity. AI helps you learn in this and makes the process a bit faster so you can focus on experimenting, creating, and making something uniquely yours (aka your own first game).

ArcadeAi for Clubs

ArcadeAi is a Hack Club program where you/your members build games and get rewarded. Run it as a workshop at your next meeting no experience needed, and it works for complete beginners. Clubs are welcome from anywhere in the world!

Running a workshop

Host an ArcadeAi workshop at your club meeting. Members will build their own game using an AI (as per the guide), host it on GitHub Pages, and submit it. Each approved submission earns them rewards. You can use our workshop guide to run the session.

How it works

1

Run the workshop with your club

2

Members build & host their games

3

Close your workshop

4

Get your reward grant!

ArcadeAi for Individuals

Building solo? No problem. ArcadeAi is open to any teenager who wants to build a game and get rewarded for shipping it. No club needed — just you, your code, and your ideas.

How it works

Build a game (time tracked with Hackatime), host it on GitHub Pages or itch.io, and submit it. Each approved submission earns you rewards. Check out the requirements & examples page first.

Rules & Guidelines

Requirements

  • Be between 13–18 years old.
  • Do not be banned from Hackatime.
  • Track at least 1 hour of work.
  • Your submission must be your own work.
  • Only one submission per participant.

Submission Checklist

  • Join the Hack Club Slack.
  • Verify your identity with Hack Club Auth.
  • Track your coding time using Hackatime.
  • Host your finished game online.
  • Submit a working game.
